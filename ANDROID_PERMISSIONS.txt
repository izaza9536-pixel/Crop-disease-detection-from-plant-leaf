VOICE ASSISTANT - one extra step on your PC (for the mic)

Open this file in Notepad:
  D:\crop_disease_app\android\app\src\main\AndroidManifest.xml

Just ABOVE the line  <application  paste these two lines:

    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO"/>
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET"/>

Save the file. Then run:
  flutter pub get
  flutter build apk

First time you press the mic in the AI Assistant, the phone will ask
"Allow CropCare AI to record audio?" - tap Allow.
The spoken answer (voice reply) uses the phone's own text-to-speech;
Urdu voice works if the phone has a Urdu TTS voice installed
(Settings > Accessibility / Text-to-speech). English voice works everywhere.
